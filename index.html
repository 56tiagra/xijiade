Short note: Simple low‑cost promotion ideas for new small local operators

Many new small local businesses in NSW start with very limited marketing budget.
A few practical, low‑cost directions work well:

  1. Clarify who your core customer group is first, before spending on posts.
  2. Build a simple regular social posting rhythm focused on what value you offer.
  3. Run small trial promotions to test response, instead of one big expensive campaign.
  4. Connect with other complementary local small businesses for cross‑referral.

Xi Jiade provides independent promotion planning & advertising concept advice for other small operators.
